Two things done properly.

We opened two years ago in an old picture-framing shop on Chalk Lane. The floors were uneven, the wiring was questionable, but out through the back door stood a decades-old fig tree yielding sweetest green fruit every August.

We decided against an expansive thirty-page menu. Instead, we committed to two humble crafts: baking sourdough loaves that crackle when pinched, and gently roasting small batches of sweet, clean filter coffee. No shortcuts, no pre-mixes, and very little compromise.

bakery_dining Mara • Head Baker

Mara Lindqvist

In at 05:40 AM

Mara runs the back kitchen with quiet discipline. Her morning sourdough undergoes a 36-hour cold ferment using a starter nicknamed "Old Barnaby." By the time the shutters roll open at 7:30, the cooling wire is lined with dark country loaves, laminated morning buns, and seasonal stone-fruit tarts.

coffee_maker Dev Patel • Roaster

Dev Patel

Twice weekly roasts

Dev watches the cast-iron vintage drum roaster installed right behind the front till. He buys directly from small producer co-ops in Huila, Yirgacheffe, and Nariño. Dev rejects harsh dark roasts in favour of high sweetness, clean acidity, and bright stone-fruit profiles that drink cleanly without milk.
