Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions

How do dietary requirements work?

We provide separately sealed and labelled gluten-free, vegan, nut-free, and dairy-free platters at no extra surcharge. Just mention specifics in your note.

What happens to the coffee carafes?

Our driver swings by anytime after 2:00pm or the next morning to pick up the empty insulated carafes and boxes. You do not need to wash them.

Can we customize the quantities?

Yes. Feel free to combine packages (e.g. Morning Tea food with extra Full Spread cold brew carafes). We tailor quotes individually.
